evolve-backtest

A toy trading strategy (src/strategy.js) on synthetic prices, scored by its Sharpe ratio: 2015–2021 for the search, 2022–2024 held out.

It is a fixture for the Evolve Arena's strategy-backtest preset on iomarkets.dev, and this page is what a live preview of it serves.

Not investment advice; the prices are generated, not a real market.
